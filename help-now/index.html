Need help now?

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 000. If you're not in danger but things feel too much, you can call one of these lines. They're free and confidential, and you don't need a diagnosis or a referral.

In danger or need to talk now?

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 000.

  • 000 Emergency (police, ambulance, fire)
  • 13 11 14 Lifeline (24/7)
  • 1300 659 467 Suicide Call Back Service (24/7)
  • 1300 22 4636 Beyond Blue
  • 13 92 76 13YARN (Aboriginal & Torres Strait Islander crisis support)
  • 1300 555 788 WA Mental Health Emergency Response Line: Perth metro
  • 1800 676 822 MHERL: Peel
  • 1800 552 002 Rurallink: country WA (after hours, weekends)
  • (08) 9442 5000 WA Alcohol and Drug Support Line (24/7): metro
  • 1800 198 024 Alcohol and Drug Support Line: country

Other WA lines

  • Here For You (mental health, alcohol and other drugs, 7am to 10pm daily): 1800 437 348. WA Mental Health Commission.
  • Parent and Family Drug Support Line (24/7): metro (08) 9442 5050, country 1800 653 203. Official page.
  • Medicare Mental Health phone line: 1800 595 212. WAPHA. Medicare Mental Health Centres are not a crisis service.
