About this guide

This guide is made by someone in Perth with lived experience of BPD, who found that working out where to go in WA was harder than it should be.

In danger or need to talk now?

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 000.

  • 000 Emergency (police, ambulance, fire)
  • 13 11 14 Lifeline (24/7)
  • 1300 659 467 Suicide Call Back Service (24/7)
  • 1300 22 4636 Beyond Blue
  • 13 92 76 13YARN (Aboriginal & Torres Strait Islander crisis support)
  • 1300 555 788 WA Mental Health Emergency Response Line: Perth metro

What this guide is, and isn't

  • It is a directory of services and how to reach them, drawn from each service's own official information.
  • It isn't a health service, and it doesn't give medical advice, diagnose anything, or recommend one treatment or provider over another.
  • It's independent and not affiliated with any provider. There are no ads, paid listings or affiliate links.
  • It's built with AI assistance. Every service detail links to its source with a "last checked" date.
