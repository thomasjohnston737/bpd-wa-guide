BPD and DBT services in Western Australia

Programs and supports in WA for people with BPD and their families. Each listing gives who it's for, how to get in, and the cost and wait if the service publishes them. Services that aren't BPD-specific, like alcohol and drug support and general mental health services, are included because people with BPD are often referred through them.

In danger or need to talk now?

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 000.

  • Youth Axis (Youth Mental Health, NMHS)

    BPD / DBT specificPublicYouth

    Who it's for
    Young people aged 16 to 24 in stable accommodation in the Perth metro area, who haven't had extensive specialist treatment, with features of emotionally unstable personality disorder (or ultra-high risk of psychosis). Time-limited: up to six months.
    How to get in
    Through the Youth Mental Health triage line, 1300 362 569 (Mon to Fri, 8.30am to 4.30pm), or the referral form emailed to youthmhtriage@health.wa.gov.au.
    Cost
    Not published
    Waitlist

  • Abbotsford Private Hospital: DBT program

    BPD / DBT specificPrivate

    Who it's for
    A voluntary private hospital for people over 16. Its DBT program is described as being for people 'struggling with emotional dysregulation'. It also runs addiction recovery groups.

    A GP or specialist referral, followed by an assessment with one of the hospital's psychiatrists. Assessments team: 0430 709 459. Email abbotsford@aurorahealth.com.au.
    Cost
    You need private health insurance, DVA, WorkCover, or to self-fund. Out-of-pocket costs are confirmed by the hospital; prices not published.
    Waitlist
    Not published

  • Marian Centre: DBT day program and BPD inpatient care

    BPD / DBT specificPrivate

    Who it's for
    The Marian Centre runs a 12-week day-patient DBT program for people diagnosed with BPD, plus inpatient care. Its day program page also lists a DBT-informed program (12 weeks weekly, or 6 weeks twice weekly).
    How to get in
    Admission must be under a psychiatrist with admitting rights at the Marian Centre. A GP or specialist refers you. Day-program dates: mrn.tlo@aurorahealth.com.au. Admissions team: 1800 540 388.
    Cost
    You need private health insurance, DVA, WorkCover, or to self-fund. Out-of-pocket costs are confirmed by admissions; prices not published.
    Waitlist
    The centre publishes an approximate wait of 1 to 6 weeks for day programs, depending on the program.
    Where
    187 Cambridge Street, Wembley WA 6016.
    Phone
    (08) 9380 4999

    Source: Marian Centre: BPD, Day Patient Programs and Referral Process · Last checked 28 Oct 2026

  • Ramsay Clinic Hollywood: DBT Day Program

    BPD / DBT specificPrivate

    Who it's for
    The clinic says it's for people with 'frequent and intense changes in their emotional state often leading to self-damaging behaviours'. One day a week for 13 weeks, with an optional 13-week advanced program after.
    How to get in
    GPs can refer directly. You don't need to have seen a psychiatrist first. A free intake assessment (about 1 hour) is done in person or by telehealth.
    Cost
    The clinic says fees are usually fully covered by private health insurance or a DVA Gold Card, or you can self-fund. Self-funded price not published.
    Waitlist
    Not published
    Where
    Hollywood Private Hospital, Monash Avenue, Nedlands WA 6009.
    Phone

  • Lifeline WA DBTeen

    BPD / DBT specificCommunityYouth

    Who it's for
    Young people aged 14 to 18 who struggle to manage emotions and behaviour, together with a parent or adult carer they live with, who must attend all 12 sessions. Not for young people currently getting intensive hospital or community mental health care.
    How to get in
    Anyone can refer, including self-referral, a parent, a GP or a school, using the online form. Then there's an information session and an assessment.
    Cost
    Free (funded by WAPHA)
    Waitlist
    Not published. Intake is by semester: referrals are open for Semester 1 2027, with groups starting in the second week of February 2027.
    Where
    Groups in Bentley, Landsdale, Yangebup, Rockingham and Shenton Park (evenings or Saturday mornings; may change).
    Phone
    (08) 9261 4498

    Source: Lifeline WA: DBTeen Groups · Last checked 28 Oct 2026
