Finding BPD and DBT support in Western Australia

A plain-English guide to services in WA for people living with borderline personality disorder (BPD), and for the people who care about them. It covers who each service is for, how to get referred, and the cost and wait where the service publishes them.

In danger or need to talk now?

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 000.

  • 000 Emergency (police, ambulance, fire)
  • 13 11 14 Lifeline (24/7)
  • 1300 659 467 Suicide Call Back Service (24/7)
  • 1300 22 4636 Beyond Blue
  • 13 92 76 13YARN (Aboriginal & Torres Strait Islander crisis support)
  • 1300 555 788 WA Mental Health Emergency Response Line: Perth metro

Who this is for

  • People in WA with a BPD diagnosis, or who think BPD might fit, looking for support.
  • Partners, parents, family and friends trying to work out where to turn.
  • Anyone trying to make sense of public, private, youth and community options in one place.

How the information is checked

Every service listing links to its official page, with the date we last checked it. If a service doesn't publish its cost or waitlist, we show "Not published" rather than guess. This guide doesn't recommend one service over another.

Please read: this is an independent guide. It is not a health service and doesn't give medical advice, diagnosis or treatment recommendations. It is not affiliated with any provider listed, and no provider has paid to be here. Details change, so always check with the service before you go. The site is built and maintained with AI assistance and checked against each service's official page on the "last checked" date shown.
